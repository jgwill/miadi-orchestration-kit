# Relations of Mia’s return to an episode take

- **Came from** Episode 339’s companion loop for Pi. The Claude Code plugin keeps the take
  receipt checks and uses its own listener and developmental-editor agent.
- **Relies on** the episode capture manifest and its transcript receipts. A take with
  missing or mismatched evidence waits for a valid capture.
- **Walks beside** the film plugin. Mia’s reply can inform the editor’s review while the
  choice of what to keep stays with the editor.
- **Holds a decision** with the person. The reply brings the episode’s evidence back
  while the person decides what to do with it.
