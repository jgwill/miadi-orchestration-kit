# 02 — Intent

## Desired outcome

A maker can perform once in a consent-bounded field session. T10 keeps the
devices aligned and the media local. T8's adapter emits a replayable receipt.
T9 and the composer may derive or refuse a cue. T6 may place an accepted cue on
an editor-controlled guide. T3 witnesses the human experience. T1 leaves the
relation resumable after every session disappears.

## Current reality

The specification, adapter and practice can be implemented and rehearsed now.
The real Episode 120 take, participant authority, composition, artistic
acceptance and timeline decision have not occurred and cannot be manufactured
by an agent.

## Structural tension

The film wants to receive the life of a gesture, while custody and creative
authority require that neither the recording nor its meaning be extracted by
the transport that relates it to music and picture.

## Boundaries

- Use current devices before proposing procurement.
- Use existing recorders and the existing film/composition toolchain.
- Keep raw media device-local or in ignored capture storage.
- Carry portable references, sha256 digests and observed technical facts.
- Require explicit authority for export, publication, participant naming,
  derived instruments and new uses.
- Class every claim as observed, human-chosen, proposed or desired future.
- Formalize T10's permanent lead only after the pilot evidence exists.

## Human-held decisions

- Participant or delegated keeper: consent, custody and permitted derivation.
- Creator/composer: whether the take is useful musical material and what music
  it becomes.
- Editor/director: whether and where a cue enters the film.
- Independent evaluator: whether technical evidence is reliable.
- Factory humans: whether T10 becomes permanent and who leads it.
