## Accountable-circle gates

| Circle | Gate question |
|---|---|
| Story | Does the scene-to-score relation serve the living narrative, and did the story keeper accept it? |
| Stewardship | Is consent explicit, custody intact and the proposed use within authority? |
| Engineering | Can the receipt, rates, drift, hashes, channels and replay be verified without media leakage? |
| Evaluation | Did a person separately judge reliability and artistic usefulness? |
| Continuity | Can another agent resume from identities, evidence, open choices and the named next transition? |

These gates coordinate teams. They do not permanently map a legacy team to a
circle.
