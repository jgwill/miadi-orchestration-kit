# Episode 120 expressive score pilot

Issue: [jgwill/miadi-orchestration-kit#157](https://github.com/jgwill/miadi-orchestration-kit/issues/157)

## Desired outcome

One embodied act captured with consent becomes musical material a composer can
shape and a film editor can place, while raw media stays in its maker's custody
and every technical transition can be replayed from references and hashes.

This pilot introduces T10, Embodied Performance & Capture, as a complement to
T8. It also preserves two useful meanings of **field-kit**:

- the physical devices and on-set practice owned by T10;
- the analogous Episode 120 App Workspace that holds the relations around a
  take without holding its media.

`@miadi/expressive-take` is the thin receipt between those places. It is not a
recorder, an App Workspace or a creative authority.

## Acceptance

The specification is implemented when the adapter, field-kit profile,
on-set-performance skill, team inventory and child-readable teaching agree on
the same boundaries. The **pilot** is complete only after a real field session
produces one verified take, linked composition, timeline placement, human
decision and replayable receipt. No specification or test substitutes for
those human and field events.
