# 01 — Reverse engineer

## Observed ancestors

- Episode 120 already models a relational film-production workspace, ScoreCue
  and SoundSession. It is the pilot coordinator, not a new recorder.
- Episode 044 distinguishes observation, first-person experience, analogy,
  proposal and desired future. Its exit-transition practice names the keeper,
  guard, target state and residual uncertainty.
- Episode 575 holds two verified Kdenlive scene stringouts and an editing-room
  sheet. Its room timeline is intentionally empty so an editor makes the cuts;
  raw captures remain outside Git.
- Episode 257's PT1 says the music Episode 044's film asks for comes from Shared
  Spark. [jgwill/Miadi#1035](https://github.com/jgwill/Miadi/issues/1035)
  records the desired first passage: after Mietta's shared-meaning clip, a
  musical desire travels from the film workspace toward the composer's
  workspace and returns as an Episode 120 ScoreCue.
- T8 already owns software development, phone reliability and film-toolchain
  dependencies. Existing capture, movement-score, composition, take and
  timeline identities must be joined rather than replaced.
- T3, T5, T6, T8 and T1 work already has active history. The pilot consumes
  those lanes' results and does not open duplicates.

## Current gap

The Factory can record media, describe movement, compose music and prepare film
timelines, but it has no small portable receipt that proves which consented
embodied take became which composition and guide. Hardware placement, clock
alignment, local fallback and media custody also have no single accountable
field practice.

## Constraints inherited from the ancestors

1. A wish for music is not a composed cue; a proposed cue is not an accepted
   cue; an accepted cue is not an editor's placement.
2. The participant's experience is not reduced to a process record.
3. Raw media, participant words, participant names and secrets do not enter Git
   or the expressive-take receipt.
4. The editor, composer, participant/keeper and independent evaluator each keep
   a distinct human decision.
5. The five accountable circles gate the passage but are not permanent aliases
   for legacy T1–T10 teams.
