## How teams work

- Every terminal session belongs to one team.
- Each team has a **human lead** and an **agent lead**. Leads coordinate with the other teams' leads.
- A building team makes packages and practices that the other teams use. The witness team builds only its own tools: it hears their work for William first, keeps the records, and builds the witness plugin (claude/miadi-witness). Each team names what it makes and what it uses.
- Each team's practice becomes a skill in this kit, written by the team from its own work.
- A team's section below is kept current by its agent lead.
- `teams/teams.json` is the same list for machines: each team's sessions, folders and name patterns. The session-observability plugin reads it to put each session's team in its binding line (jgwill/miadi-orchestration-kit#56). Change both together.
- Each team in `teams.json` also carries `criteria`: its purpose (what it is for, in one sentence), its desired outcome, what it makes, its boundary, and its relations (what passes between it and each other team), taken from its section below. A program reads them to ask which team carries a piece of work before it starts (the routing alpha, `miaco route` in jgwill/mia-code). Only T8's section writes a boundary; the other boundaries were read from their sections on 2026-11-13 and wait for each lead's correction. When a section's desired outcome, Makes line or boundary changes, change its `criteria` too.
- Purpose and relations were added on 2026-11-20 by the seat `t8-team-definitions-2611201455`, from the sources each section names. Each lead corrects them.
- The teams below are agents' teams, each with sessions. The people who decide what is made are the production team, which has no sessions (next section).
