## 1. One gesture can become part of a film

Subtitle: The recording stays with its keeper.

Visual: a hand-drawn gesture line moving toward a film frame while the original
recording remains inside a safe circle.

## 2. One name, two field-kits

- Physical field-kit: bodies, devices, clocks, monitoring and local custody.
- App Workspace: episodes, consent, composition, timeline and handoff relations.
- Thin bridge: the expressive-take receipt.

## 3. The receipt carries facts, not recordings

Carries: references, hashes, requested/observed rates, drift, channel state and
guides. Leaves behind: audio/video/image bytes, participant words/names,
credentials and creative authority.

## 4. The living passage

Perform → keep media local → verify receipt → composer chooses → editor places.
At every arrow, consent can say hold or stop.

## 5. Four people keep four choices

- participant/keeper: permitted use;
- independent evaluator: technical reliability;
- creator/composer: musical usefulness and composition;
- editor/director: film placement.

## 6. Ten teams support one passage

Foreground: T10 field practice, T8 adapter, T9 story, T6 composition/timeline.
Support ring: T1 continuity, T2 events, T3 witness, T4 chart, T5 context, T7
operator desk. Five circles are gates, not renamed teams.

## 7. Rehearse, then prove

Episode 575: verified stringouts, reference-only rehearsal, no media copied.
Episode 120: one real consented field take. Episode 257 Shared Spark PT1 and
Miadi#1035: creative lineage and MusicalDesire, not yet an accepted cue.

## 8. Five signs it lived

One verified take. One linked composition. One accepted timeline placement.
One recorded human decision set. One replayable receipt and resumable handoff.

Final action: “The recording stays with its keeper; the receipt carries safe
facts; people decide what the gesture becomes.”
