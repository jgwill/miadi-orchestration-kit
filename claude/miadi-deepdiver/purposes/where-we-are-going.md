# Purpose: where we are going

Guillaume, 2026-11-10: "another purpose of another video type ... is to help know where we're going. So there really is a revision of the script and something to create a very great narrative so that when you listen to that video, you see where we're going. And that part implies that you have access to the whole ecosystems and probably even to some of the sessions, events, and the context and the capability inside of the actual system."

**Who it serves**: the teams and the community who will build what comes next. It is made to be played back in a screenwalk: Guillaume stops it, comments aloud, and that screenwalk becomes an `explainer-feedback` review, whose points the `explainer-feedback-loop` skill (miadi-witness) sends to the repositories that own them. The video is the first half of that loop.

**Form**: a Video Overview, `--format explainer`, stopped at its plan (`--review`). Its plan is read as the script: a narrative with stops a person can pause on.

**Sources** (the episode, not one session):
- the episode's `goal:`: the desired outcome, which the narrative arrives at
- its specifications (`rispecs/`), its threads (`threads.yaml`: each wanted result and its current reality) and its open charts (`stc/`, `cnarrative delayed --all`)
- the inventory records and digests of the sessions that worked on it (`episode:` in `~/workspace/.mino/session-inventory/*.json`)
- the capabilities it names, from the capability registry (Episode 568, `rispecs/capability-registry/`)
- the reviews of its screenwalks, read last

**Instruction** (fill each `<…>`, keep the rest as written):

```text
Where <episode title> is going. Start from what exists today, each piece with its proof: <the capabilities, commits and records it rests on>. Then show, as scenes, the moments the episode's goal creates and that do not exist yet: <each part of the goal, as one person or agent doing one thing>. For each scene, say what the work before it needs, which team or session would make it, and what would show it happened: <from the threads' wanted results, the open chart steps and the sessions' missions>. Make each scene a stop where a listener can pause and say what they would change. Say plainly what is proposed and not built. Speak only in the language of creating: the desired outcome, current reality, the next step.
```

**Before sending the plan on**: the plan is a sequence of scenes that arrive at the goal, not a status list; each scene names who makes it and what proves it; nothing proposed is presented as built.
