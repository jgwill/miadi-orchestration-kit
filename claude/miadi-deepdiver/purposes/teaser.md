# Purpose: a teaser, fiction on real facts

Guillaume, 2026-11-10: "take on the whole transcript and everything that had happened and say, okay, if I need to put this in a fiction writing, I'm not going to do a 6-minute video. I will want something shorter. And in that fiction, the whole session and the whole work that was done would try to use all of the feature capabilities and what the platform is going to be capable of doing with all of that, and would put this in a teaser that we can actually listen to ... So it starts with how it had evolved and then where we are. So it makes something really amazing and fictitious, but based on real facts."

**Who it serves**: anyone who should want what the work makes possible, the community first. Write the audience in `perspectives.md`, never in the instruction.

**Form**: a Video Overview, `--format short`, stopped at its plan (`--review`). Short enough to listen to once.

**Claim category**: fiction, kept apart from what was observed. The real part says what happened and where it stands, with its proofs; the fiction part is one scene, and every capability in it either exists or is named as coming by the episode's goal, its threads, its open chart steps or the session's missions. `perspectives.md` and the README say which part is which.

**Sources**: those of "what happened" (the session's inventory record and digest, the recording's transcript) and those of "where we are going" (the episode's goal, specifications, threads and charts, and the capability registry).

**Instruction** (fill each `<…>`, keep the rest as written):

```text
A teaser, a fiction built on real facts, about what <session or episode> makes possible. Open with how the work evolved, in a few real steps, each with its proof: <from the digest, the record and the transcript>. Then where it stands today: <current reality, one line>. Then one continuous scene of fiction: <a person> uses what the platform will be able to do with this work: <the capabilities, built or named as coming by the goal, the threads, the chart steps and the missions>. Every capability in the scene is one of these; nothing else is invented. Close on what can be done with the platform today. Keep it short. Speak only in the language of creating: what is being created, current reality, the next step.
```

**Before sending the plan on**: the plan moves from real steps to one scene; each capability in the scene is in the list given; the real part claims nothing the evidence does not show.
